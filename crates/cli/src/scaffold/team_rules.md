# Team rules

Here: how this team works, in every repo bound to it — what each of its workers holds to, whatever its role.
Not here: what one codebase asks, which is that repo's own rules to say, and what one kind of worker does, which is its role's.

The host user's own skills and rules may be loaded beside these, unless the team file says `host_user: false`. Where they disagree, your brief, your role's rules, these rules and the repo's own rules win over the host user's, and a stage ends only as your brief says.
