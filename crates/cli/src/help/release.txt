WHEN TO RUN IT

  Hardly ever: `close`, `escalate` and `park` release the claim they end, and a landed
  PR's claim is released by the tick. Run it to drop a claim made with `claim`, or one
  left behind by something that went wrong.

WHO RUNS IT

  The operator, in a checkout of the repo.

WHAT IT READS AND WRITES

  It deletes the issue's claim on the remote, only while it is still the claim that was
  read. It does not read or change GitHub, and touches no worker and no worktree.

WHAT IT ANSWERS

  {"released": true, "issue", "ref"} — also when the issue had no claim: already gone
  counts as released.

  Errors: the claim is another instance's ("is not this fleet's claim"); it "moved since
  it was read" — somebody took it meanwhile, and it was left alone; the delete failed.

WHAT TRIPS YOU UP

  - `--instance` is required, and must be the instance that holds the claim. To end
    another instance's claim, take it first: `takeover`.
  - It only unlocks. The issue keeps its labels, its worker keeps working, and — still
    open and ready — the issue is back on the frontier: a fleet dispatches it again.
