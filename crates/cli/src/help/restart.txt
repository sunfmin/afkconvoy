WHEN TO RUN IT

  To replace a silent worker by hand. A tick does this on its own, once, for a claim
  whose PR is open and not on the landing train, and whose worker stopped, was nudged,
  and stayed silent. Do it yourself when a plan keeps showing such a claim under `mine`
  as `joining`.

WHO RUNS IT

  The operator, in a checkout of the repo, on the host the worker runs on.

WHAT IT READS AND WRITES

  It records the restart in the fleet's comment on the PR, then starts a new worker in
  the same worktree, briefed only to join the landing train. The PR, the branch and the
  worktree are untouched.

WHAT IT ANSWERS

  {"issue", "pr", "action": "restarted", "restarted", "terminal", "prompt"} — restarted
  is the time, in epoch seconds; prompt is the brief the new worker got.

  Errors, each with nothing changed: "has no stopped worker" — the instance holds no
  claim on the issue, its worker is at work or gone, or the issue has no open PR; "is
  not restarted" — the PR's join already had its restart, the worker was not nudged yet,
  or it showed a sign of life within the grace period; the claim is another instance's.

WHAT TRIPS YOU UP

  - `--instance` is required, and must be the instance that holds the claim.
  - One restart per PR. A worker silent again after it is escalated by the next tick.
  - It is not `dispatch`: for a claim with no PR, continue it with `dispatch`.
