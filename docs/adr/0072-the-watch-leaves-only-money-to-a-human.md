# The watch leaves only money to a human

Every watch, stock or in the operator's directory, leaves only spending money with a human. It lists each such decision with its question and answer line and acts on nothing in it.

Everything else the watch decides by the recommended answer and acts on: what cannot be undone, taking a secret or private data off the host, and changing what a product is for or who it is for. It records each decision in its report's `decided` — what it chose, why, and how to undo it — and the round's daybook entry carries it under `Did` and `Undo`. Before something that cannot be undone, it first makes what way back it can: a backup, the old copy kept.

A step that needs a person's hands — an account, a credential, a login, a click — holds nothing else back. The watch goes on with everything else and lists that one step for the asking session; only the work that needs that step waits.

A prohibition — a hook, a guard, a role's prohibition — that guards money still needs a human to loosen it. The watch may loosen any other, recorded in `decided` the same way.

This amends ADR-0036's four kinds left to a human. Source: [control's ADR 0006](https://github.com/sunfmin/control/blob/master/docs/adr/0006-agents-answer-escalations-felix-keeps-four-kinds.md) and sunfmin/control#815.
